Initial release of Libravault.

• EPUB reader powered by Readium — dark, light, and sepia themes
• PDF viewer with paginated and scrolling modes
• Audiobook player for MP3, M4B, OGG, FLAC, Opus, and AAC
• Chapter navigation with sleep timer and 10-second fade-out
• Scoped Storage — no broad file permissions ever requested
• No account, no internet, no tracking
