LibraVault is a modern, unified, privacy-first media reader for Android.

One secure vault for all your books and audio — read EPUBs, view PDFs, and listen to audiobooks in a single app, with zero unnecessary permissions and zero required accounts.

FEATURES
• EPUB reader with font, size, theme and scroll controls
• PDF viewer with pinch-to-zoom and paginated or scroll mode
• Audiobook player (MP3, M4B, OGG, FLAC, Opus, AAC) with chapter navigation, sleep timer and variable speed
• Scoped Storage only — you choose which folders the app can see, nothing else
• Bookmarks and highlights for EPUB and PDF
• Dark, light and sepia reading themes
• Full offline — no network connection ever required
• No account, no cloud sync, no telemetry, no ads

PRIVACY
LibraVault never requests READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. File access is granted only for the folders you explicitly select via Android's Storage Access Framework. No data ever leaves your device.
