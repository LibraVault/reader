Libravault is a modern, privacy-first media reader for Android. One secure vault for all your books and audio — with zero unnecessary file permissions and zero required accounts.

Unlike existing apps, Libravault never requests broad storage access. You choose exactly which folders it can see. Nothing else on your device is ever accessed.

<b>Supported formats</b>
• EPUB — full Readium-powered rendering with font, theme, and layout controls
• PDF — native Android PDF viewer with pinch-to-zoom and paginated mode
• Audiobooks — MP3, M4B, OGG, FLAC, Opus, AAC with chapter navigation

<b>Reader features</b>
• Dark, light, and sepia reading themes
• Adjustable font size, font family, and line spacing
• Paginated or continuous scroll mode
• Tap zones for one-handed page navigation
• Bookmarks and highlights (EPUB and PDF)
• Last-position restore — silently, with no dialogs

<b>Player features</b>
• Chapter navigation for M4B and ID3v2 chapter-tagged MP3s
• Variable playback speed from 0.5× to 3.0×
• Sleep timer with 6 presets, end-of-chapter option, and 10-second fade-out
• Lock screen and notification controls
• Android Auto ready
• Position saved every 5 seconds — never lose your place

<b>Privacy</b>
• No internet permission — ever
• No account required
• No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting to remote servers
• Optional local-only crash logging — stored on your device, never transmitted
• Scoped Storage: you grant access to specific folders only
• 100% open source (GPL-3.0) — verify every claim yourself

<b>Library</b>
• Instant library load from local cache — no waiting on startup
• Background scan on first launch; pull-to-refresh for updates
• Search by title, author, narrator, or series
• "Continue reading" and "Continue listening" cards on the home screen

Libravault is free on F-Droid. A one-time purchase is available on Google Play. No subscription. No in-app purchases. No ads.
